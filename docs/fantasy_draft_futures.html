Statistical modelling

Jaiden has built models to estimate player value over for the next 5 seasons. This is designed for use in AFL Fantasy keeper leagues or dynasty leagues. You can customise the season to find the ideal players based on how you think your premiership window feels. If you are in ‘win-now’ mode, you can choose the current season. If you are rebuilding, perhaps 5 years from now is your target.

For SuperCoach players, I intend to add its scoring layer at some point, but all metrics related to contracts & expected games are still relevant.

All models are based on over a decade of data that is collected from public sources. I do not own any of the data presented. Player data, weekly injury lists, and player images are collected from the AFL website. Fantasy positions are sourced from the official AFL Fantasy platform. Contract updates come from the AFL website and its affiliated club websites. List updates come from Draftguru.

  • pListed is the probability that this player is still on an AFL list as-of the chosen season.

  • xGames is the number of games the player is expected to play up to the end of the chosen season.

  • xAvg is the predicted Fantasy average for this player in the chosen season.

  • xValue is the expected total fantasy points this player will score up to the end of the chosen season. You can see movers in the Rising & Falling section.

  • VORP is “value over replacement player” based on your imported league squad size & number of teams. This compares the scarcity of each position in your league. For example, a keeper league with 2 ruck spots requires more rucks to be listed, so a replacement from free agents might not even be playing.

  • Age is the player’s current age.

  • Contract is the player’s current contract. I update these manually, so email me if you find something outdated. Third-party websites have a lot of errors, so I prefer official AFL articles.

  • Pos column is the player’s positional future for fantasy perspective. It is modelled separately and used to calculate VORP for each future horizon. For example, when viewing many years into the future, the position splits represent the proportion of those seasons with DEF, MID, RUC, FWD status respectively. For current season, it will be the player’s current position(s) plus the chance of gaining a new one. It is assumed that players can be assigned up to 3 unique positions at one time as per the official AFL Fantasy platform.
